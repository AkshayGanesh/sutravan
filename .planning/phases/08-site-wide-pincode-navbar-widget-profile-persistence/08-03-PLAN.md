## Phase Goal (MVP user story)

**As a** Sutravan shopper, **I want to** set or change my delivery pincode from a navbar
widget on any page — with the choice remembered site-wide (and tied to my account when I'm
logged in), **so that** I never re-enter it between pages or devices and every delivery
estimate reflects where I actually am.

**This plan verifies the whole story is true end-to-end.**
