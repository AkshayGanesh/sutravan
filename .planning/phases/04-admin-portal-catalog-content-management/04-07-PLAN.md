<objective>
Deliver the site-content vertical slice: an admin edits hero copy, Our Story body (rich text), contact email, and social URLs in the portal — and those edits replace the hardcoded, duplicated strings across the public site, live (ADMIN-05/06). Rich text is authored with TipTap and rendered safely through DOMPurify.

Purpose: The email/social links and hero/Our Story copy are currently hardcoded in 7 files (D-20). This slice moves them to the site_content-backed single source so one edit updates every location together, with a mandatory code-default fallback so nothing renders blank. The Our Story rich text is the one dangerouslySetInnerHTML path in the app — it goes through the Plan-02 sanitizer (XSS contract).
Output: SiteContent editor page + lazy RichTextEditor + rewire of Footer/Navbar/Contact/ProductDetail/Shop (email + social) and Hero/OurStory (copy) to useSiteContent with fallbacks.
</objective>
